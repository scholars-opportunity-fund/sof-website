SOF modifications: retained cortical, cerebellar, and brainstem geometry; grouped it into five website navigation regions; centered and uniformly scaled it; welded shared vertices, softened tessellation with four paired Taubin smoothing passes, and recomputed normals; replaced original materials with SOF visual styling. The connection network is a decorative navigation metaphor.
The upstream source and license are retained in design/brain/source. This model license applies to the anatomical asset; it does not relicense the website's application code.
