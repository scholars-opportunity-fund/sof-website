The U.S. equity market is the most heavily instrumented capital market in
history. Nearly every dollar of float in a large-cap name is touched by a
systematic participant within any given trading day. Coverage, capital, and
algorithmic participation collapse onto the same few hundred tickers.

Special situations are the exception.

Spin-offs, forced selling around index reconstitutions, post-reorganization
equities, odd-lot tenders, rights offerings, and stub situations all share
two features: the pricing signal is set by non-economic sellers, and the
analytical work required to underwrite the opposite side is rarely worth the
time of a senior portfolio manager at a multi-manager fund. The result is a
pocket of the market where patient, rigorous, bottom-up analysis still moves
the needle.

## What we actually do

SOF is not a generalist long-only fund. Our process is narrow by design:

1. **Detect.** A proprietary platform ingests regulatory filings (8-Ks, S-1s,
   S-4s, 13-Ds, proxies) continuously, scoring each against a set of feature
   weights calibrated to the situations we've found most fertile.
2. **Review.** Candidates clearing the score threshold move into structured
   fundamental analysis. Student analysts build a collective memo. The memo
   is torn apart internally before it ever reaches the CIO.
3. **Decide.** Dr. Brogaard makes every investment decision. Position sizing,
   entry, and exit stay with him. Student work ends at the memo.

That separation matters. It lets us run a high-throughput analytical engine
with student labor without introducing uncontrolled investment risk. Every
step from detection to outcome is documented and auditable.

## Why this is a good time

Two forces push in our direction. First, dislocation in the active long-only
community continues to drive capital out of fundamental coverage and into
systematic vehicles, leaving situations that require judgment (not factor
tilt) increasingly underpriced. Second, the quality of regulatory data
available for systematic ingestion has improved materially, making detection
cheaper and faster than it was even five years ago.

Neither trend reverses quickly. We intend to be early and patient.
